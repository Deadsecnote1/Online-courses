## 1. Copywriting Best Practices & Rules
1. **Highlight "100% FREE" & "$0" Boldly:** Always emphasize that no credit card is required.
2. **Scarcity & Urgency:** Explicitly state that coupons expire in 24–48 hours or after 1,000 redemptions.
3. **Direct Web Link:** Drive users to `courses.domain.com/course/{slug}` or specific category landing pages to ensure ad impressions are generated upon arrival (See [[Architecture - Monetization and Ad Placements#1-google-adsense-container-specifications--ad-placement-zones|Ad Impression Placement]]).
4. **Clean Formatting:** Use emojis, bold headers, and short bullet points for high readability on mobile screens.

## 4. UTM Parameter Tracking Matrix
Use standardized UTM tags across all broadcast links to track channel performance in Google Analytics & Rakuten reporting:

| Parameter | Telegram Channel | WhatsApp Broadcast | Newsletter / Email |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `utm_source` | `telegram` | `whatsapp` | `newsletter` |
| `utm_medium` | `channel` / `group` | `broadcast` / `community` | `email_digest` |
| `utm_campaign` | `daily_drop` / `niche_alert` | `daily_drop` / `weekend_pack` | `weekly_roundup` |
